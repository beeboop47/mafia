New role

Special win conditions are checked independently of the side's normal win condition.
Win logic: Evil wins when the number of living evil players is at least the number of living non-evil players. Good wins when no evil players remain. Neutral roles remain in play after evil is eliminated; their own win conditions are checked when applicable. A role with the 'Wins when voted out' condition ends the game when that player is voted out. If evil is eliminated while a Jester is still alive, the Jester becomes a Serial Killer instead of the game ending.
