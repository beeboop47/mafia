# Mafia

A customisable Mafia game for friends, with private roles, day voting, night abilities and special reactions to said abilities.

Play together using one shared device, or create an online room so everyone can use their own device. This is a personally crafted version of Mafia with its own rules and unusual roles. You can edit the role library to suit your group.

## How to play

1. **Set up the game.** Add players, choose a role preset or configure your own role pool.
2. **Receive your role privately.** Pass the shared device between players, or view your role on your own device in an online room.
3. **Discuss and vote during the day.** Decide who you suspect and vote to eliminate a player.
4. **Use abilities at night.** Depending on your role, investigate, protect, attack, deceive or change other players.
5. **Resolve night reactions.** Some roles can respond to attacks, survive encounters, revive players or receive information.
6. **Continue until a win condition is met.**

Keep your private screen hidden from other players. Discussion, bluffing and persuasion are part of the game.

## Sides and roles

Roles belong to three alignments:

- **Good:** work together to identify and eliminate Evil.
- **Evil:** deceive the group and take control of the surviving players.
- **Neutral:** follow your role’s objectives; these can affect how the game ends.

The role library includes familiar roles such as Citizen, Mafia and Detective, alongside roles such as:

| Role | Ability |
| --- | --- |
| Seer | Learns a selected player’s exact role each night. |
| Gambler | Receives a different night ability. |
| Policeman | Receives permanent intelligence about one player. |
| Hater | Has a permanent target; Evil wins if that target is voted out. |
| Doctor | Can revive a player killed that night, once per game. |
| Leader | Can permanently replace another player’s role. |

Read your role description and the game prompts for its specific rules.

## Role assignment

**Random mode** assigns roles from the configured pool. Selected Evil role counts are guaranteed first, subject to available player slots. Remaining slots draw from the pool, then fill with Citizens.

**Manual mode** lets you assign specific players specific roles. Leave a player assigned **None** to have their role chosen from the random pool. Manual assignments count towards that pool’s role counts and Evil minimums.

## Private host setup

Manual mode includes a collapsible host panel with settings for **individual players**:

- Choose each Seer’s target for each night.
- Choose each Gambler’s ability for each night.
- Choose each Policeman’s intelligence target.
- Choose each Hater’s permanent target.
- Add mutual or one-way recognition so selected players privately know each other’s roles.

Two players with the same role can have completely different settings. Unconfigured schedules stay random. If a scheduled Seer target is no longer alive, a random eligible living player is selected.

These settings control assignments and information. Players still choose their own actions and targets for abilities that require a choice.

## Make it your own

Expand **Advanced role configuration** to change the random pool or browse the role library. Create, edit or delete roles, and configure their abilities, reactions, visibility and special win conditions.

You can also choose the night order, adjust automatic side-win checks, save custom role-pool presets, and save or export your full configuration.
