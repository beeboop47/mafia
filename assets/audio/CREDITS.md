`suburban-night-source.mp3` — “Ambience - outdoors at night, suburban, with crickets.wav” by **jodybruchon**. General suburban outdoor ambience with crickets, distant car traffic and heating/air-system hum.

`suburban-night-loop.wav` is derived from the 26-second recording: stereo 44.1 kHz PCM, gently filtered above 3.5 kHz to soften insects, with a three-second overlap joining the end and beginning. The resulting 23-second loop has no encoder padding or added silence. Playback uses a quieter mix and retains three-second day/night transitions.
